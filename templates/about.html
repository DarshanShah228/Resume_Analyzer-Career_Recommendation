ABOUT CAREERAI

Build a Stronger Career with Smarter Resume Insights

CareerAI helps students and job seekers understand their resume strengths, identify skill gaps, compare their profile with job descriptions, and receive personalized career guidance.

WHAT IS CAREERAI?

Your Career Intelligence Assistant

CareerAI is a career-support platform designed to turn resume and job-description data into practical career insights.

Instead of simply checking whether a resume is good or bad, CareerAI helps users understand what they already know, what they are missing for a target role, and what they can do next to improve their career readiness.

CareerAI focuses on
📄 Resume understanding and analysis
🎯 Target-role skill matching
💼 Job-description comparison
🚀 Actionable career recommendations

PROJECT ARCHITECTURE

How the System Works

CareerAI combines a web interface with backend analysis and recommendation components to process resume and job-related information.

Frontend → Flask API → Resume Analyzer → Skill Matching → Recommendation Engine → Database
